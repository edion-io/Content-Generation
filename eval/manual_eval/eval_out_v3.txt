<exercise>Alvin had 20 oranges. He gave some oranges to Roy and had 8 left. Roy had 16 oranges. How many oranges did Alvin give to Roy?

Let \( x \) represent the number of oranges Alvin gave to Roy. Set up an equation based on the given information and solve for \( x \).

\begin{equation*}
20 - x = 8
\end{equation*}

Solving for \( x \) gives:

\begin{equation*}
x = 20 - 8
\end{equation*}

\begin{equation*}
x = 12
\end{equation*}

Alvin gave Roy 12 oranges.</exercise>

\end{enumerate}</exercise>
